* [QuakerSpeak videos](https://quakerspeak.com/), for instance [this one](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I63xA-VZGXw), are an excellent resource.
* Letters to a fellow seeker by Steve Chase,

  * [Chapter 2](/assets/PDF/Letters-to-a-fellow-seeker/Steve_Chase_Letters-to-a-fellow-seeker-ch1.pdf)

## Quaker organizations & Quaker work

* ✶ [Canadian Friends Service Committee](https://quakerservice.ca): Quakers in Canada actively working on peace, indigenous issues, and a more healing criminal justice system.
* ✶ [Quaker United Nations Office](https://quno.org/), working in New York City and Geneva on peacebuilding, climate change, refugees, and a just economy.
* 🍁 [Canadian Yearly Meeting](https://www.quaker.ca), the national organization of the Canadian Quakers (of which we are a part), and their [Organization and Procedure](https://quaker.ca/resources/organization-and-procedure/).
* [The Canadian Friend](https://quaker.ca/resources/the-canadian-friend/) - the magazine of Canadian Yearly Meeting, available online.
* [Canadian Young Friends Yearly Meeting](https://quaker.ca/cyfym/), focused on Young Friends (13-18) and Young Adult Friends (18-35).
* [Kairos](https://www.kairoscanada.org/), interfaith action focused on ecology and Indigenous and migrant rights.
* [Quaker Earthcare Witness](https://www.quakerearthcare.org/) ([video](https://youtu.be/5GBZUEeX1M0))
