Over countless generations, our encounter with Spirit has led us over and over to these six core values:

<Icon name="fa-regular:circle" class="color-1-dark-text down_a_bit_more" style="width: 2em; height: 2em; display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;" /> &nbsp;**S**implicity. Simple living helps us focus on what matters most.

<Icon name="fa-solid:dove" class="color-1-light-text down_a_bit_more" style="width: 2em; height: 2em; display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;" /> &nbsp;**P**eace. Quakers have a long history of building peace. 

<Icon name="fa-solid:handshake" class="color-1-dark-text down_a_bit_more" style="width: 2em; height: 2em; display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;" /> &nbsp;**I**ntegrity. Speaking and living truth as best we can.

<Icon name="fa-solid:comments" class="color-1-text down_a_bit_more" style="width: 2em; height: 2em; display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;" /> &nbsp;**C**ommunity. Valuing our ties to others.

<Icon name="fa-brands:creative-commons-nd" class="color-1-light-text down_a_bit_more" style="width: 2em; height: 2em; display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;" /> &nbsp;**E**quality. Seeing the equal createdness of everything that is.

<Icon name="fa-brands:pagelines" class="leaf down_a_bit_more" style="width: 2em; height: 2em; display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;" /> &nbsp;**S**tewardship. Respect and gratitude for what sustains us.

<br />

These are not rules to be imposed; rather, our traditional, simple — yet quite challenging — values represent our experience in Quaker worship. 

They are our testimony of how Spirit calls us to live in this world. 

*************

<Icon name="fa-solid:heart" class="heart down_a_bit_more" style="width: 2em; height: 2em; display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;" /> &nbsp;The greatest gift which we have all been given is Love. Love is at the origin of our commitment to human rights, prison abolition, sustainability, and peace.
