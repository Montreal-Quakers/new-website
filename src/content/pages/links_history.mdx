  * <a href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B8iS545He0DEcmV2NFhHb1J2ak0/view" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Chapter 1</a>

## Quaker organizations & Quaker work

* ✶ <a href="https://quakerservice.ca" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Canadian Friends Service Committee</a>: Quakers in Canada actively working on peace, indigenous issues, and a more healing criminal justice system.
* ✶ <a href="https://quno.org/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Quaker United Nations Office</a>, working in New York City and Geneva on peacebuilding, climate change, refugees, and a just economy.
* 🍁 <a href="https://www.quaker.ca" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Canadian Yearly Meeting</a>, the national organization of the Canadian Quakers (of which we are a part), and their <a href="https://quaker.ca/resources/organization-and-procedure/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Organization and Procedure</a>.
* <a href="https://quaker.ca/resources/the-canadian-friend/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">The Canadian Friend</a> - the magazine of Canadian Yearly Meeting, available online.
* <a href="https://quaker.ca/cyfym/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Canadian Young Friends Yearly Meeting</a>, focused on Young Friends (13-18) and Young Adult Friends (18-35).
* <a href="https://www.kairoscanada.org/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Kairos</a>, interfaith action focused on ecology and Indigenous and migrant rights.
* <a href="https://www.quakerearthcare.org/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Quaker Earthcare Witness</a> (<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5GBZUEeX1M0" rel="noopener" target="_blank">video</a>)
